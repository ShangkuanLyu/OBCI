2025年8月8日，大洋洲工商业委员会在墨尔本世贸中心举行中医药大健康产业推荐会暨工商会年中酒会，澳中各界200余位嘉宾出席。会长Ken Smith回顾了近期工作，介绍了会员赴苏州参展的机会及委员会向新西兰、斐济、库克群岛、基里巴斯、瑙鲁、巴布亚新几内亚、萨摩亚、所罗门群岛、汤加、图瓦卢、瓦努阿图等大洋洲国家拓展的计划。

执行会长Sunny Sun通报了代表团访华情况、苏州大洋洲产业园概况，以及10月26日至27日将在墨尔本举办的澳中健康产品博览会与世界传统医药论坛的筹备情况。皇家儿童医院基金会介绍了“Fly for the Kids”项目。

维州议会前上议长Bruce Atkinson当选委员会新任会长；新一届理事会成立并颁发证书，并聘任荣誉顾问；委员会新办公室揭牌，博览会组委会同日宣布成立。
<!-- body:en -->
On 8 August 2025 the Oceania Business Council held its TCM and Health Industry Promotion Meeting and mid-year reception at World Trade Centre Melbourne, attended by more than 200 guests from the Australian and Chinese communities. President Ken Smith reviewed the council's recent work, noted an opportunity for members to exhibit in Suzhou, and outlined the council's expansion to other Oceania countries, including New Zealand, Fiji, the Cook Islands, Kiribati, Nauru, Papua New Guinea, Samoa, the Solomon Islands, Tonga, Tuvalu and Vanuatu.

Executive President Sunny Sun reported on the delegation's visit to China, an overview of the Oceania Industrial Park in Suzhou, and preparations for the Australia–China Health Products Expo and the World Traditional Medicine Forum, to be held in Melbourne on 26–27 October 2025. The Royal Children's Hospital Foundation presented its "Fly for the Kids" project.

Former President of the Victorian Legislative Council Bruce Atkinson was elected the council's new President. A new council was formed, with certificates presented and honorary advisers appointed; the council's new office was unveiled, and the expo organising committee was announced on the same day.
