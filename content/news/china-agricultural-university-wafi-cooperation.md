12月11日，继成功访问北京保利艺术博物馆后，大洋洲工商业委员会执行会长、澳大利亚内蒙古总商会创会会长孙国照率代表团一行，前往中国农业大学，与该校院长、WaFi平台总策划付文阁教授举行深度工作会谈。双方聚焦农业食品与大健康产业，围绕国际平台共建、活动联动、产业融合等议题展开务实交流，共同擘画中澳在相关领域互利合作的新蓝图。

会谈在富有建设性的氛围中进行。付文阁院长首先对孙国照会长一行的到访表示欢迎，并系统介绍了WaFi平台的定位与发展愿景。WaFi作为一个高规格的国际交流与展示平台，旨在汇聚全球智慧与资源，推动农业、食品与大健康产业的创新融合与可持续发展，其顾问委员会成员包括前农业部部长等权威人士，平台秉持“农食一体，药食同源”的先进理念，致力于构建全球产业协同网络。

孙国照会长详细阐述了大洋洲工商业委员会在促进澳中乃至大洋洲地区与中国经贸文化交流中所扮演的桥梁角色。他特别表达了对WaFi平台理念与价值的高度认同，并代表委员会正式提出强烈意愿，希望争取成为WaFi平台“澳大利亚（大洋洲）专场”的主要主办方之一。孙会长表示，委员会将全力利用其在地资源优势，积极推介并组织澳大拉西亚地区优质的农业科技、绿色食品、健康产品及相关企业参与WaFi平台活动，同时积极推动WaFi大洋洲办事处的建立，提议以澳大利亚文化经济重镇墨尔本作为基地，以便更高效、更常态化的服务区域合作与资源对接。

双方就一系列具体合作事项达成重要共识与初步规划：

- **平台深度参与：**大洋洲工商业委员会将积极组织参与2026年3月举办的WaFi平台重要活动，并力争在活动中呈现具有大洋洲特色的专题内容，展示该地区在农业与食品健康领域的技术成果与产业优势。
- **双向考察联动：**作为回访与深度对接，中国农业大学MBA项目师生及关联企业代表团计划于2026年11月赴澳大利亚，参与当地重要的大健康产业展会，并同步开展为期10天左右的商务考察与交流活动。此举旨在让中国产业界与学界精英深入澳洲本土，实地了解市场、对接技术、探索合作。
- **理念与实践融合：**双方一致同意，将共同倡导与践行“农食一体，药食同源”的理念，通过WaFi等平台，推动从田间到餐桌、从农产品到健康品的全产业链合作，促进中澳在高端农业、功能食品、健康管理等领域的产学研协同创新。

此次会谈成果丰硕，标志着大洋洲工商业委员会与中国农业大学的合作从意向沟通迈入了具体规划与项目推进的新阶段。双方一致认为，农业与健康是关乎民生的永恒产业，也是中澳互补优势显著、合作潜力巨大的重点领域。最终，双方共同展望，愿以此次会晤为起点，携手共促中澳友谊，推动两国在经贸、人文、教育等方面的共同发展，特别是通过WaFi这样的高端平台，将两国的产业优势、科技资源与市场潜力紧密结合，为全球农食健康产业的可持续发展贡献“中澳方案”。

随着合作蓝图的徐徐展开，大洋洲工商业委员会与中国农业大学的此次战略携手，不仅为WaFi平台的国际化、区域化运作注入了强劲动力，也为中澳在农业与大健康这一关键领域的务实合作搭建了专业化、机制化的新桥梁，必将为两国企业和人民带来更多实实在在的福祉与发展机遇。

On 11 December, following a successful visit to the Poly Art Museum in Beijing, Sunny Sun, Executive President of the Oceania Business Council and Founding President of the Australia Inner Mongolia General Chamber of Commerce, led a delegation to China Agricultural University for in-depth working talks with Professor Fu Wenge, Dean at the university and chief planner of the WaFi platform. Focusing on the agri-food and health industries, the two sides held practical discussions on jointly building an international platform, linking events and integrating industries, and together outlined a new blueprint for mutually beneficial China-Australia cooperation in these fields.

The talks took place in a constructive atmosphere. Dean Fu Wenge first welcomed President Sunny Sun and his delegation and gave a systematic introduction to the positioning and development vision of the WaFi platform. As a high-level international exchange and exhibition platform, WaFi aims to bring together global expertise and resources to drive innovative integration and sustainable development across the agriculture, food and health industries. Its advisory committee includes authoritative figures such as a former Minister of Agriculture, and the platform upholds the forward-looking concept of "agriculture and food as one, with medicine and food sharing the same origin", dedicating itself to building a global network of industry collaboration.

President Sunny Sun explained in detail the bridging role that the Oceania Business Council plays in promoting economic, trade and cultural exchanges between Australia, and the wider Oceania region, and China. He expressed strong agreement with the concept and values of the WaFi platform and, on behalf of the Council, formally conveyed a strong wish to become one of the principal hosts of a WaFi "Australia (Oceania) Session". President Sun said the Council would make full use of its local resources to actively promote and organise the participation of high-quality agricultural technology, green food and health product enterprises from Australasia in WaFi platform events, and would actively promote the establishment of a WaFi Oceania office, proposing Melbourne, a major cultural and economic centre of Australia, as its base so as to serve regional cooperation and resource matching more efficiently and on a regular basis.

The two sides reached important consensus and preliminary plans on a series of specific cooperation matters:

- **In-depth platform participation:** The Oceania Business Council will actively organise participation in the major WaFi platform event to be held in March 2026 and will strive to present themed content with distinctive Oceania characteristics, showcasing the region's technological achievements and industrial strengths in agriculture, food and health.
- **Two-way study visits:** As a return visit and deeper engagement, a delegation of faculty and students from the China Agricultural University MBA programme, together with representatives of affiliated enterprises, plans to travel to Australia in November 2026 to attend a major local health industry exhibition and to carry out business study and exchange activities of around 10 days. The aim is to bring leading figures from Chinese industry and academia into Australia to understand the market first-hand, connect with technology and explore cooperation.
- **Integrating concept and practice:** The two sides agreed to jointly advocate and put into practice the concept of "agriculture and food as one, with medicine and food sharing the same origin", using WaFi and other platforms to promote whole-industry-chain cooperation from field to table and from agricultural products to health products, and to foster industry-academia-research collaborative innovation between China and Australia in high-end agriculture, functional foods and health management.

The talks were highly productive and mark the transition of cooperation between the Oceania Business Council and China Agricultural University from an exchange of intentions to a new stage of concrete planning and project implementation. Both sides agreed that agriculture and health are enduring industries that concern people's livelihoods, and are also priority fields in which China and Australia enjoy clear complementary strengths and great potential for cooperation. Looking ahead, both sides expressed the hope that this meeting would serve as a starting point for jointly advancing China-Australia friendship and promoting the shared development of the two countries in trade, people-to-people exchange and education, and in particular for closely combining the industrial strengths, scientific resources and market potential of both countries through high-level platforms such as WaFi, contributing a "China-Australia solution" to the sustainable development of the global agri-food and health industry.

As the blueprint for cooperation gradually unfolds, this strategic partnership between the Oceania Business Council and China Agricultural University not only injects strong momentum into the international and regional operation of the WaFi platform, but also builds a new professional and institutionalised bridge for practical China-Australia cooperation in the key fields of agriculture and health, and will surely bring more tangible benefits and development opportunities to the enterprises and people of both countries.
