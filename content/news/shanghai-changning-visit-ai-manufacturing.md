一杯由机器人冲泡的咖啡，一场围绕科技与未来的对话，大洋洲与中国商贸合作在上海长宁区写下新的注脚。

12月13日，大洋洲工商业委员会代表团孙国照会长一行到访上海长宁区，展开了一场科技与创新的深度体验。代表团参观了氦豚机器人科技有限公司的咖啡机器人、了解WELLOCEAN的全局化人工智能港口解决方案，并与长宁区科委签署战略合作协议。

这场访问展现了中国在人工智能与高端制造领域的最新成果，也为大洋洲与中国企业的合作开辟了新路径。

## 咖啡机器人：一杯饮品的科技革命

在氦豚机器人科技有限公司，代表团目睹了如何通过人工智能技术重新定义咖啡体验。COFE+第6代全智能·咖啡机器人展示了其卓越能力：50秒完成一杯现磨咖啡，单日产能突破1000杯。

![COFE+ 咖啡机器人现场制作的咖啡，奶泡上 3D 打印的图案](01.jpg)

这款机器人不仅速度惊人，更具备大师级拉花技艺和3D打印功能，能将企业Logo、照片甚至游客笑脸瞬间印制在咖啡奶泡上。代表团成员品尝了由机器人制作的咖啡，对其精准的口感和艺术性表示赞叹。

氦豚科技作为全球饮品机器人领域的领军企业，拥有100多项国际专利，其产品已通过美国FDA、欧盟CE等全球权威认证。COFE+已成功进入全球50多个发达国家，覆盖中国15个省市，落地场景包括景区、商场、高校、医院等上千类点位。这种轻资产、高效率、高毛利的模式为餐饮行业提供了全新解决方案。

## 智慧港口：人工智能赋能全球贸易

代表团随后来到WELLOCEAN，WELLOCEAN代表向代表团介绍，该系统是“全球首套多功能人工智能远控岸桥识别系统”，具备地面识别方式、多功能同步实时识别以及全时段、全天候高识别准确率等特点。

![WELLOCEAN 智慧港口解决方案展示](02.jpg)

物联网技术联通“人、车、箱、船、机、场”六大要素，使提送箱在港作业时间缩短26.2%，远洋干线船舶在泊船时效率提升28%。系统串联起码头多台岸桥、场桥和人工智能运输车，涵盖多个人工智能算法和数个智能模块。运输车在北斗导航系统指引下到达智能解锁站，然后沿着大数据计算的最佳行驶路线把货物运到指定位置。相信未来的合作领域将会更加的广阔。

## 战略签约：构建长期合作机制

参观结束后，大洋洲工商业委员会代表团与上海长宁区科委代表周开悦共同出席了在PoBe德必产业园举行的战略合作协议签署仪式。长宁区作为上海开发开放早、国际化程度高的区域，始终致力于为企业提供高效便捷的服务。此次与大洋洲工商业委员会的合作，是长宁区践行“相聚上海 共创未来”全球宣介活动精神的又一具体举措。

![大洋洲工商业委员会与上海长宁区科委战略合作协议签署仪式](03.jpg)

这一合作框架将建立常态化交流机制，推动大洋洲企业与长宁区在人工智能、高端制造等领域的深度合作。合作内容涵盖技术交流、人才培训、项目对接等多个层面。

## 合作前景：大洋洲与中国企业的共赢之路

大洋洲工商业委员会代表团此次长宁之行，为双方未来的合作奠定了坚实基础。随着全球竞争格局重塑，企业出海不再只是“走出去”，而是以技术为底座、以合规为战略的系统工程。

大洋洲企业可以借助长宁区的平台优势，更好地进入中国市场；中国企业也可以通过这一通道，拓展大洋洲市场。随着战略合作协议的签署，上海市区长宁区与大洋洲企业的合作将进入新阶段。

A cup of coffee brewed by a robot and a conversation about technology and the future: in Shanghai's Changning District, business cooperation between Oceania and China gained a new chapter.

On 13 December, an Oceania Business Council delegation led by President Sunny Sun visited Changning District, Shanghai, for an in-depth experience of technology and innovation. The delegation toured the coffee robots of Helium Dolphin Robotics Technology Co., Ltd., learned about WELLOCEAN's holistic artificial intelligence port solution, and signed a strategic cooperation agreement with the Changning District Science and Technology Commission.

The visit showcased China's latest achievements in artificial intelligence and advanced manufacturing, and opened new avenues for cooperation between Oceanian and Chinese enterprises.

## Coffee Robots: A Technological Revolution in a Single Cup

At Helium Dolphin Robotics Technology Co., Ltd., the delegation saw how artificial intelligence is redefining the coffee experience. The sixth-generation COFE+ fully intelligent coffee robot demonstrated its capabilities: a freshly ground cup of coffee in 50 seconds, with a daily output exceeding 1,000 cups.

![Coffee made on site by the COFE+ coffee robot, with a design 3D-printed on the milk foam](01.jpg)

The robot is not only remarkably fast but also masters latte art and 3D printing, instantly printing company logos, photographs and even visitors' smiling faces onto the milk foam. Delegation members tasted coffee made by the robot and praised its precise flavour and artistry.

As a leading global company in beverage robotics, Helium Dolphin holds more than 100 international patents, and its products have passed authoritative certifications worldwide, including the US FDA and EU CE. COFE+ has entered more than 50 developed countries around the world and covers 15 provinces and municipalities in China, deployed at over a thousand types of locations including scenic areas, shopping malls, universities and hospitals. This asset-light, high-efficiency, high-margin model offers the food and beverage industry an entirely new solution.

## Smart Ports: Artificial Intelligence Empowering Global Trade

The delegation then visited WELLOCEAN, where a WELLOCEAN representative explained that the system is "the world's first multifunctional AI remote-controlled quay crane recognition system", featuring ground-based recognition, synchronous real-time multifunctional recognition, and high recognition accuracy around the clock in all weather conditions.

![Display of WELLOCEAN's smart-port solution](02.jpg)

Internet of Things technology links the six elements of "people, vehicles, containers, ships, machinery and yards", cutting container pick-up and delivery time at port by 26.2% and raising the berth efficiency of ocean-going mainline vessels by 28%. The system connects multiple quay cranes, yard cranes and AI transport vehicles at the terminal, incorporating a number of AI algorithms and several intelligent modules. Guided by the BeiDou navigation system, the transport vehicles travel to intelligent unlocking stations and then carry the cargo to its designated position along the optimal route calculated from big data. It is believed that the scope for future cooperation will be even broader.

## Strategic Signing: Building a Long-Term Cooperation Mechanism

After the tours, the Oceania Business Council delegation and Zhou Kaiyue, representative of the Shanghai Changning District Science and Technology Commission, attended the signing ceremony for a strategic cooperation agreement held at the PoBe DoBe Industrial Park. As one of the earliest areas of Shanghai to open up and one of its most internationalised, Changning District has always been committed to providing efficient and convenient services for enterprises. This cooperation with the Oceania Business Council is another concrete step by Changning District in carrying forward the spirit of the "Meet in Shanghai, Create the Future Together" global promotion campaign.

![Signing ceremony for the strategic cooperation agreement between the Oceania Business Council and the Shanghai Changning District Science and Technology Commission](03.jpg)

This cooperation framework will establish a regular exchange mechanism and promote in-depth cooperation between Oceanian enterprises and Changning District in fields such as artificial intelligence and advanced manufacturing. The cooperation covers multiple areas, including technology exchange, talent training and project matchmaking.

## Cooperation Outlook: A Win-Win Path for Oceanian and Chinese Enterprises

The Oceania Business Council delegation's visit to Changning has laid a solid foundation for future cooperation between the two sides. As the global competitive landscape is reshaped, going overseas is no longer simply about "going out"; it is a systematic undertaking built on technology and guided by a compliance strategy.

Oceanian enterprises can draw on Changning District's platform advantages to enter the Chinese market more effectively, while Chinese enterprises can use this channel to expand into the Oceanian market. With the signing of the strategic cooperation agreement, cooperation between Shanghai's Changning District and Oceanian enterprises will enter a new stage.
