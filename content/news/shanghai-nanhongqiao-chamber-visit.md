一次深入的交流，一杯香醇的咖啡，为大洋洲与中国企业合作开辟了新路径。

2025年12月5日，大洋洲工商业委员会代表团孙国照会长一行到访上海，与南虹桥商会举行友好交流会议，上海市闵行区人民政府新虹街道办事处副书记周行君出席会议。南虹桥商会外联部李晓详细介绍了其发展定位、组织架构、会员情况以及赋能企业出海的举措，双方共同探讨了未来互利合作方向。此次访问进一步加强了大洋洲工商业委员会与中国商协会机构的联系，为促进大洋洲与中国的经贸往来搭建了新平台。

![大洋洲工商业委员会代表团一行到访南虹桥商会](01.jpg)

## 南虹桥商会：国际化商会的能级跃升

南虹桥商会在交流中全面展示了其作为国际化商会的全新定位。今年4月，该商会完成了能级提升的重要变革，更名为“南虹桥商会”，并扩大了会员规模，优化了组织架构。

商会会员有147家知名企业，形成了更加开放、包容和国际化的商会氛围。在组织架构上，商会设立了出海服务联盟和青年创业者联谊会，构建了“政治引领承载体、社会治理联合体、建设发展共同体”三位一体的组织体系。

![交流会上介绍的南虹桥商会会员情况](02.jpg)

## 全方位服务体系：助力企业全球发展

南虹桥商会构建了多元化的会员服务体系，尤其在赋能企业出海方面提供了有力支持。商会依托虹桥国际中央商务区的区位优势和政策优势，为企业搭建完善的出海服务平台。

出海服务联盟作为开放、共享、协同的服务平台，整合多方资源，为企业提供包括跨境财税规划、海外上市辅导等专业化服务。

商会还定期举办“企业出海”系列主题活动，组织会员企业参访成功出海企业，学习品牌全球化经验。这些活动为会员企业搭建了深度交流平台，共同探寻在数字化运营、海外市场拓展等方面的合作机遇。

![交流会议现场](03.jpg)

## 咖啡文化：商务交流的独特纽带

在本次交流活动中，初心咖啡负责人张泽华成为双方沟通的独特桥梁。咖啡文化作为一种跨语种、跨国界的共识载体，在商务交流中发挥着越来越重要的作用。

南虹桥商会所在的虹桥国际中央商务区核心区，正在打造更加开放包容的国际营商环境。咖啡文化交流正是这种国际化氛围的体现。

![交流现场的咖啡杯，杯套印有「抵达上海的第一杯」](04.jpg)

## 上海内蒙古商会：蒙澳携手共筑健康桥梁

上海内蒙古商会执行会长方铭贤与大洋洲工商业委员会执行会长、澳大利亚内蒙古总商会创会会长孙国照在上海举行会晤。双方围绕中澳医疗康养产业合作、资源共享等议题进行了深入交流。

双方一致同意建立常态化交流机制，未来将依托澳中健康产业博览会等平台，推动内蒙古康养资源与国际市场对接，深化在医疗、康养等领域的务实合作。

## 共赢未来：拓展大洋洲与中国合作空间

本次交流为双方未来合作奠定了坚实基础。大洋洲工商业委员会孙国照会长一行作为推动大洋洲国家与中国经贸合作的非营利性组织，其核心目标是响应“一带一路”倡议，推动大洋洲国家与中国的经贸往来。

交流结束后，双方代表均表示将建立长期交流互访机制。南虹桥商会将组织企业家代表团回访大洋洲，实地考察澳大利亚和新西兰的市场环境与合作机遇。

大洋洲工商业委员会也计划在未来一年内组织更多大洋洲企业访问上海，利用虹桥国际中央商务区的平台优势，进一步拓展中国市场。

An in-depth exchange and a cup of fragrant coffee opened up a new path for cooperation between enterprises in Oceania and China.

On 5 December 2025, a delegation of the Oceania Business Council led by President Sunny Sun visited Shanghai and held a friendly exchange meeting with the Nanhongqiao Chamber of Commerce. Zhou Xingjun, Deputy Party Secretary of the Xinhong Sub-district Office of the People's Government of Minhang District, Shanghai, attended the meeting. Li Xiao of the External Relations Department of the Nanhongqiao Chamber of Commerce gave a detailed introduction to the chamber's development positioning, organisational structure, membership and measures to empower enterprises going global, and the two sides jointly explored directions for future mutually beneficial cooperation. The visit further strengthened the Oceania Business Council's ties with Chinese chambers of commerce and business associations and built a new platform for promoting economic and trade exchanges between Oceania and China.

![The Oceania Business Council delegation arriving at the Nanhongqiao Chamber of Commerce](01.jpg)

## Nanhongqiao Chamber of Commerce: An International Chamber Steps Up a Level

During the exchange, the Nanhongqiao Chamber of Commerce gave a full presentation of its new positioning as an international chamber of commerce. In April this year, the chamber completed a major transformation to raise its standing, was renamed the "Nanhongqiao Chamber of Commerce", expanded its membership and optimised its organisational structure.

The chamber's members comprise 147 well-known enterprises, creating a more open, inclusive and international chamber atmosphere. In terms of organisational structure, the chamber has established a Going-Global Service Alliance and a Young Entrepreneurs Association, building a three-in-one organisational system of "a carrier of political leadership, a union for social governance and a community for construction and development".

![The chamber's membership presented at the meeting](02.jpg)

## A Comprehensive Service System: Supporting Enterprises' Global Development

The Nanhongqiao Chamber of Commerce has built a diversified member service system, providing strong support in particular for empowering enterprises going global. Drawing on the locational and policy advantages of the Hongqiao International Central Business District, the chamber has built a comprehensive going-global service platform for enterprises.

As an open, shared and collaborative service platform, the Going-Global Service Alliance integrates resources from many parties to provide enterprises with professional services including cross-border tax and financial planning and guidance on overseas listings.

The chamber also regularly holds a series of themed "Enterprises Going Global" events, organising member enterprises to visit companies that have successfully gone global and learn from their experience in taking brands international. These events give member enterprises a platform for in-depth exchange, jointly exploring cooperation opportunities in areas such as digital operations and overseas market expansion.

![The exchange meeting in progress](03.jpg)

## Coffee Culture: A Unique Bond in Business Exchange

During this exchange, Zhang Zehua, head of Chuxin Coffee, became a unique bridge for communication between the two sides. Coffee culture, as a shared medium that crosses languages and borders, is playing an increasingly important role in business exchange.

The core area of the Hongqiao International Central Business District, where the Nanhongqiao Chamber of Commerce is located, is building a more open and inclusive international business environment. The exchange of coffee culture is an embodiment of this international atmosphere.

![A coffee cup at the meeting, its sleeve printed with "the first cup on arriving in Shanghai"](04.jpg)

## Shanghai Inner Mongolia Chamber of Commerce: Inner Mongolia and Australia Join Hands to Build a Health Bridge

Fang Mingxian, Executive President of the Shanghai Inner Mongolia Chamber of Commerce, met in Shanghai with Sunny Sun, Executive President of the Oceania Business Council and founding President of the Australia Inner Mongolia General Chamber of Commerce. The two sides held in-depth exchanges on topics including China–Australia cooperation in the medical and health and wellness industries and the sharing of resources.

The two sides agreed to establish a regular exchange mechanism and, in future, to draw on platforms such as the Australia–China Health Industry Expo to connect Inner Mongolia's health and wellness resources with international markets and deepen practical cooperation in fields such as medical care and health and wellness.

## A Win-Win Future: Expanding the Space for Cooperation Between Oceania and China

This exchange laid a solid foundation for future cooperation between the two sides. The Oceania Business Council, represented by President Sunny Sun and his delegation, is a non-profit organisation that promotes economic and trade cooperation between the countries of Oceania and China; its core objective is to respond to the Belt and Road Initiative and promote economic and trade exchanges between the countries of Oceania and China.

After the exchange, representatives of both sides said they would establish a long-term mechanism for exchanges and reciprocal visits. The Nanhongqiao Chamber of Commerce will organise a delegation of entrepreneurs to pay a return visit to Oceania to examine first-hand the market environment and cooperation opportunities in Australia and New Zealand.

The Oceania Business Council also plans to organise more Oceania enterprises to visit Shanghai within the coming year, using the platform advantages of the Hongqiao International Central Business District to further expand into the Chinese market.
