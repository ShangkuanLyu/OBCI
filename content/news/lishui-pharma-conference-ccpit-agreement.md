2025年11月23日，第十九届成长型医药企业发展大会在浙江丽水开幕。澳大利亚大洋洲工商业委员会会长孙国照率团参会，展示马努卡蜂蜜等健康产品，并与丽水市政府开展交流。

会议期间，委员会与丽水市贸促会签署战略合作协议，建立长期合作关系。双方达成三项合作：一是邀请丽水医药企业组团参加2026年10月在墨尔本举办的澳中健康产业博览会，丽水市将提供出海补助；二是推动在青田进口商品城设立大洋洲馆，引进澳大利亚产品；三是代表团实地考察丽水本地医药企业，促进产业对接。

此次合作为两地医药健康产业搭建了双向贸易与交流平台。
<!-- body:en -->
On 23 November 2025, the 19th Growth-Stage Pharmaceutical Enterprises Development Conference opened in Lishui, Zhejiang. Sunny Sun, President of the Oceania Business Council of Australia, led a delegation to the conference, showcased health products such as manuka honey, and held exchanges with the Lishui Municipal Government.

During the conference, the Council signed a strategic cooperation agreement with the Lishui Council for the Promotion of International Trade (CCPIT), establishing a long-term cooperative relationship. The two sides agreed on three areas of cooperation: first, inviting Lishui pharmaceutical enterprises to attend as a group the Australia-China Health Industry Expo to be held in Melbourne in October 2026, with Lishui providing subsidies for going overseas; second, promoting the establishment of an Oceania Pavilion at the Qingtian Imported Commodities City to bring in Australian products; and third, arranging for the delegation to visit local pharmaceutical enterprises in Lishui on site to promote industry matchmaking.

This cooperation has built a platform for two-way trade and exchange between the pharmaceutical and health industries of the two regions.
