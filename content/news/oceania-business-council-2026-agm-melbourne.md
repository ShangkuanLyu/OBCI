5月1日星期五，暮春时节的墨尔本生机盎然。在地标性建筑——墨尔本世界贸易中心（WTC），一场汇聚政商精英的盛会如期而至。澳大利亚大洋洲工商业委员会（Oceania Business Council, OBC）在此隆重举行2026年度会员大会。本次大会以“承前启后，活力绽放”为主题，不仅是对过去一年辉煌战绩的全面检阅，更是对未来战略转型与全球化布局的深度擘画。

![2026年度会员大会现场](01.jpg)

大会现场精英云集，高朋满座。来自中澳两国的政要、企业家、行业领袖及各界贤达逾百人出席。VIP嘉宾：Cr Gladys Liu，Mr. Simon Qian OAM，Mr. Arthur Wu，Mr. Bill Long，Mr. Nick Yan，Mr. Jerry Zhu，Hon. Ken Smith AM，Hon. Bruce Atkinson AM等，共同见证了OBC从传统商协会向“国际资源整合平台”华丽转身的里程碑时刻。

![大会现场](02.jpg)

## 高屋建瓴：核心领导层亮相与组织架构革新

大会伊始，OBC会长、Hon. Bruce Atkinson AM（维多利亚州立法议会前议长）发表了热情洋溢的开幕致辞。他高度评价了委员会在过去一年中在跨文化沟通与经贸桥梁搭建方面所做的努力，并对新一年的战略规划提出了殷切期望。

![大会开幕致辞](03.jpg)

随后，Hon. Ken Smith AM（OBC荣誉主席、创会会长）登台发言。作为OBC的缔造者，Smith先生深情回顾了委员会的创立初心，并对当前取得的体系化成果表示欣慰。大会上举行了庄重的证书颁发仪式：Hon. Ken Smith AM 为 Hon. Bruce Atkinson AM 颁发“理事会荣誉主席”证书，以表彰其在推动州际及国际事务中的卓越领导力；同时，Hon. Bruce Atkinson AM 亦为 Hon. Ken Smith AM 颁发议员证书，肯定其作为精神领袖对组织的深远影响。

![证书颁发仪式](04.jpg)

![证书颁发仪式](05.jpg)

执行会长孙国照（Sunny Sun） 代表理事会作了题为《战略转型与全球链接》的年度工作报告。他系统回顾了OBC在组织建设、国际合作及项目落地方面的突破性进展，特别是与七家机构联合发起成立“澳大利亚国际创业创新联盟”的重大举措，标志着OBC正式完成了从OBAI到OBC的品牌升级与战略重塑。

## 深耕细作：年度成果回顾与双向交流机制

在随后的会议议程中，秘书长 Sheryl Leigh 详细阐述了2026年委员会年度工作计划。她强调，OBC将不再局限于简单的牵线搭桥，而是转向“深水区”运作，构建实质性的双向交流平台和长效机制。

### 对华合作：从“签约”走向“落地”

执行会长孙国照在汇报中指出，OBC在过去一年中实现了对华合作的体系化布局。委员会已与中国浙江、江苏、安徽、上海、云南、广东、内蒙古等多个省市建立了深度战略合作机制，并与各地贸促会、商务局及行业协会建立了直通渠道。目前，合作重心已从早期的协议签署阶段，全面迈入常态化对接与实质性项目推进的新阶段。

### 标杆项目：闪耀国际舞台

会议重点展示了两项年度标杆成果：一是OBC组织会员企业参加中国全国性医药行业博览会，并成功设立“大洋洲馆”，集中展示澳洲优质大健康产品，为中国市场开拓铺平道路；二是2025年10月在墨尔本成功举办的“澳中大健康产业博览会”暨“世界传统医药论坛”。该盛会吸引了全球400余家中国企业参展及150多位专家学者与会，极大地提升了OBC的国际声誉，并促成了海量商业配对。

![大会现场](32.jpg)

### 项目落地：打造中澳经贸核心载体

备受瞩目的苏州吴中区“大洋洲产业园”项目在大会上再次被提及。目前该项目已取得战略合作共识，正稳步推进，未来将成为中澳双向投资与贸易的物理载体与核心枢纽。此外，围绕企业实际需求，OBC已建立起涵盖TGA认证、市场准入、仓储物流及本地推广的全链条服务体系，切实帮助会员企业实现商业价值闭环。

## 展望未来：构建有执行力的国际合作组织

站在新的历史起点，OBC对未来发展提出了更高远的愿景。2026年，委员会将继续秉持“链接大洋洲，服务全世界”的使命，重点做好三件事：一是扩大“朋友圈”，加速国际合作网络向亚太及其他地区延伸；二是跑出“加速度”，全力推动大洋洲产业园等重点项目从图纸变为现实；三是提供“高价值”，依托六大专业分会，为会员提供更具针对性的精准服务。

OBC正逐步成长为一个“有资源、有平台、有项目、有执行力”的新型国际组织。正如执行会长孙国照所言：“我们不仅是一个商会，更是一个生态系统。”

## 致谢与闭幕

大会尾声，特别鸣谢对委员会做出重大贡献的 Hon. Ken Smith AM 先生，正是他的远见卓识奠定了OBC的基石。同时，衷心感谢 Cr Gladys Liu、Mr. Simon Qian OAM、Mr. Arthur Wu、Mr. Bill Long、Mr. Nick Yan、Mr. Jerry Zhu 以及洪门民治党主委伍颂达、上海商会会长钱恒裕等各界领导长期以来给予OBC的鼎力支持。

最后，在世界贸易中心（WTC）代表的精彩推介后，伴随着悠扬的音乐与热烈的交谈，2026年度会员大会在温馨而充满活力的商务交流酒会中圆满落幕。这不仅是一场总结过往的盛会，更是一个凝聚共识、携手共赢的新开端。大洋洲工商业委员会正蓄势待发，准备在国际舞台上绽放更加夺目的光彩。

On Friday 1 May, Melbourne was brimming with vitality in the late-spring season. At the landmark World Trade Centre Melbourne (WTC), a gathering of political and business leaders took place as scheduled: the Oceania Business Council (OBC) of Australia held its 2026 Annual General Meeting there in grand style. Under the theme "Building on the Past, Bursting with Vitality", the meeting was not only a comprehensive review of the outstanding achievements of the past year, but also an in-depth blueprint for future strategic transformation and global expansion.

![The 2026 Annual General Meeting](01.jpg)

The venue was filled with distinguished guests. More than one hundred political figures, entrepreneurs, industry leaders and prominent members of the community from China and Australia attended. VIP guests including Cr Gladys Liu, Mr. Simon Qian OAM, Mr. Arthur Wu, Mr. Bill Long, Mr. Nick Yan, Mr. Jerry Zhu, Hon. Ken Smith AM and Hon. Bruce Atkinson AM jointly witnessed this milestone moment as the Oceania Business Council transformed from a traditional business association into an "international resource integration platform".

![The meeting in session](02.jpg)

## A Commanding Vision: The Core Leadership Takes the Stage and the Organisation Is Restructured

At the opening of the meeting, Hon. Bruce Atkinson AM, President of the Oceania Business Council and former President of the Legislative Council of Victoria, delivered a warm opening address. He spoke highly of the Council's efforts over the past year in cross-cultural communication and in building bridges for economic and trade cooperation, and expressed high expectations for the strategic plan for the year ahead.

![Opening address](03.jpg)

Hon. Ken Smith AM, Honorary Chairman and Founding President of the Oceania Business Council, then took the stage. As the founder of the Council, Mr. Smith looked back with deep feeling on the original aspirations behind its establishment and expressed his satisfaction with the systematic results achieved to date. A solemn certificate presentation ceremony was held at the meeting: Hon. Ken Smith AM presented Hon. Bruce Atkinson AM with the certificate of "Honorary Chairman of the Board" in recognition of his outstanding leadership in advancing interstate and international affairs; at the same time, Hon. Bruce Atkinson AM presented Hon. Ken Smith AM with a councillor certificate, acknowledging his profound influence on the organisation as its spiritual leader.

![Certificate presentation ceremony](04.jpg)

![Certificate presentation ceremony](05.jpg)

Executive President Sunny Sun delivered the annual work report, entitled "Strategic Transformation and Global Connectivity", on behalf of the Board. He systematically reviewed the Council's breakthroughs in organisational development, international cooperation and project implementation, in particular the major initiative of co-founding the "Australian International Entrepreneurship and Innovation Alliance" together with seven institutions, which marked the formal completion of the Council's brand upgrade and strategic reshaping from OBAI to OBC.

![Executive President Sunny Sun delivers the annual work report](06.jpg)

## Steady Cultivation: Review of the Year's Achievements and Two-Way Exchange Mechanisms

In the subsequent agenda, Secretary-General Sheryl Leigh elaborated on the Council's annual work plan for 2026. She stressed that the Council will no longer confine itself to simple matchmaking, but will move into "deep-water" operations, building substantive two-way exchange platforms and long-term mechanisms.

### Cooperation with China: From "Signing" to "Implementation"

In his report, Executive President Sunny Sun pointed out that over the past year the Council has achieved a systematic framework for cooperation with China. The Council has established in-depth strategic cooperation mechanisms with a number of Chinese provinces and municipalities, including Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui, Shanghai, Yunnan, Guangdong and Inner Mongolia, and has opened direct channels with local Councils for the Promotion of International Trade (CCPIT), commerce bureaus and industry associations. The focus of cooperation has now moved fully beyond the early stage of signing agreements into a new stage of regular engagement and the advancement of substantive projects.

### Flagship Projects: Shining on the International Stage

The meeting highlighted two flagship achievements of the year. First, the Council organised member enterprises to take part in a national pharmaceutical industry expo in China and successfully set up an "Oceania Pavilion" showcasing premium Australian health products, paving the way for the development of the Chinese market. Second, the "Australia-China Health Industry Expo" and the "World Traditional Medicine Forum" were successfully held in Melbourne in October 2025. The event attracted more than 400 Chinese enterprises from around the world as exhibitors and over 150 experts and scholars as participants, greatly enhancing the Council's international reputation and generating a large volume of business matchmaking.

![The meeting in session](32.jpg)

### Project Implementation: Building a Core Vehicle for Australia-China Trade

The much-anticipated "Oceania Industrial Park" project in Wuzhong District, Suzhou, was mentioned again at the meeting. The project has reached a consensus on strategic cooperation and is progressing steadily; in the future it will become the physical vehicle and core hub for two-way investment and trade between China and Australia. In addition, in response to the practical needs of enterprises, the Council has built a full-chain service system covering TGA certification, market access, warehousing and logistics, and local promotion, effectively helping member enterprises close the loop on commercial value.

## Looking Ahead: Building an International Cooperation Organisation with the Capacity to Deliver

Standing at a new historical starting point, the Council has set out a loftier vision for its future development. In 2026, the Council will continue to uphold its mission of "Connecting Oceania, Serving the World" and will focus on three priorities: first, expanding its "circle of friends" by accelerating the extension of its international cooperation network into the Asia-Pacific and other regions; second, "picking up speed" by pushing key projects such as the Oceania Industrial Park from blueprint to reality; and third, delivering "high value" by relying on the six professional committees to provide members with more targeted and precise services.

The Council is steadily growing into a new type of international organisation "with resources, a platform, projects and the capacity to deliver". As Executive President Sunny Sun put it: "We are not only a chamber of commerce, but an ecosystem."

## Acknowledgements and Closing

As the meeting drew to a close, special thanks were extended to Hon. Ken Smith AM for his major contributions to the Council; it was his foresight that laid the cornerstone of the Oceania Business Council. Heartfelt thanks also went to Cr Gladys Liu, Mr. Simon Qian OAM, Mr. Arthur Wu, Mr. Bill Long, Mr. Nick Yan and Mr. Jerry Zhu, as well as to Wu Songda, Chairman of the Hongmen Minzhi Party, Qian Hengyu, President of the Shanghai Chamber of Commerce, and other leaders from all walks of life for the strong support they have long given to the Council.

It was not only an occasion to review the past, but also a new beginning for building consensus and pursuing shared success. The Oceania Business Council is poised and ready to shine even more brightly on the international stage.
