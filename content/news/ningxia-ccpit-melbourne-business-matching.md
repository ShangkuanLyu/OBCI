近日，由**大洋洲工商业委员会**与**宁夏贸易促进委员会**联合主办的中澳商务对接交流会，在墨尔本Hexa Space顺利落幕。活动汇聚澳洲政府嘉宾、本土优质企业及宁夏特色产业代表团，以产业对话、品牌展示、战略签约为核心，搭建起宁澳经贸互通、资源对接、长效合作的国际化平台，为两地企业务实合作、产业出海赋能蓄力。

本次活动摒弃形式化交流，聚焦**精准对接、务实共赢、长效赋能**，通过政企致辞、产业分享、企业推介、战略合作签约、自由洽谈多个核心环节，高效打通宁夏特色产业出海通道，精准链接澳洲市场、技术与渠道资源，实现两地优势互补、双向发展。

## 大咖致辞定调 锚定中澳合作新方向

两位核心嘉宾的致辞，为两地经贸合作明确了务实深耕、长期共建的发展基调，勾勒出宁澳产业协同的清晰路径。

### 大洋洲工商业委员会：搭建中澳双向经贸桥梁

大洋洲工商业委员会执行会长孙国照先生发表开场致辞。他表示，中澳经贸互补性突出，澳洲拥有成熟的国际市场与商业体系，宁夏坐拥葡萄酒、特色农产品、大健康食品等优质特色产业，是中国西北对外开放的重要窗口。

他强调，本次对接会是两地**常态化、机制化合作的全新起点**。未来委员会将持续发挥海外平台优势，全方位助力宁夏企业深耕澳洲市场、打造国际品牌，同时引入澳洲优质资源落地宁夏，构建双向奔赴的经贸合作生态。

### 宁夏贸促会：携特色产业开拓澳洲国际市场

宁夏贸易促进委员会会长梁万荣先生重点推介了宁夏核心特色产业，聚焦**贺兰山东麓葡萄酒、枸杞农产品、绿色食品加工、大健康产业**等优势赛道，介绍了宁夏产业的生态优势与品质竞争力。

梁会长表示，澳洲是宁夏企业出海布局的核心重点市场。此次率本土头部企业代表团赴澳，旨在主动对接澳洲高端市场、先进技术与成熟贸易渠道。他期待两地建立常态化沟通机制，在贸易互通、品牌共建、市场开拓等领域深化务实合作，让宁夏优质特色产品扎根澳洲、走向国际。

## 中澳双向对话 激活产业合作新势能

本次活动集结宁夏十余标杆企业，覆盖葡萄酒、特色农食、供应链贸易、企业营销等核心领域。银色高地、留世、沃尔丰酒庄及杞里香、厚生记、百事恒兴等知名品牌，集中展示宁夏特色产品与产业实力，凭借优质品质获得澳洲政企嘉宾广泛认可，充分展现了宁夏特色产业的国际竞争力与发展潜力。

澳洲本土企业代表积极响应、精准对接，形成高效双向互动格局。

Hexa Group六合集团CEO William Yang先生结合澳洲本土运营经验分享核心观点，提出跨境合作的关键在于**本土适配、落地运营、长效发展**。作为本次活动场地支持方，六合集团深耕墨尔本本土市场，熟悉当地商业规则与消费需求。未来将持续依托场地、商圈与运营资源，为中澳企业对接洽谈、项目落地、品牌推广提供一站式支撑，助力宁夏品牌快速适配澳洲市场，解决出海落地难题。

大洋洲工商业委员会商业服务分会秘书长赵松亮，聚焦跨境商务服务、合规运营、资源整合，剖析宁澳合作机遇与痛点，为宁夏企业出海提供专业、可落地的实操指导，打通两地商业服务协同通道。

佰德芙酒庄负责人王岭梅，结合澳洲葡萄酒产业发展经验，分享国际酒庄运营、品牌出海、产区联动的成熟模式，为宁夏葡萄酒产业对标国际标准、优化产品体系、开拓海外市场提供了宝贵参考。

## 数字电商赋能 打通企业出海新通道

为助力宁夏传统产业数字化出海，活动特邀澳洲数字技术、跨境电商领域资深从业者分享实战经验，破解传统外贸渠道窄、效率低、对接难等问题，为企业国际化转型赋能。

Digitec IT Solutions总监Flex Huo聚焦数字化跨境运营、海外品牌线上推广，分享适配中小企业的出海方案，提出依托数字技术搭建线上跨境销售矩阵，助力宁夏产品精准触达澳洲终端市场。

Fureeze创始人Alex Xiao结合澳洲电商规则与消费特点，详解跨境电商流量运营、渠道布局实操路径。他表示，宁夏优质农食、红酒产品高度契合澳洲高端消费需求，借助成熟电商模式可快速打开增量市场。

同时，BERRINGA百龄佳创始人Peter Woodward、澳醇思慕集团董事总经理潘雪飞，分别分享澳洲大健康、高端食品产业的市场布局与合作模式，为宁澳多产业融合发展拓宽了合作边界。

## 战略签约落地 开启长期共赢新格局

活动现场，大洋洲工商业委员会与宁夏贸易促进委员会正式签署**战略合作备忘录（MOU）**，标志着两地正式建立长期稳定的战略合作伙伴关系。

本次签约是两地常态化深度合作的重要里程碑。未来双方将聚焦产业对接、品牌出海、技术交流、贸易互通、人才联动等核心领域，持续搭建专业化商务对接平台，推动宁夏特色产业规模化出海，助力澳洲优质资源落地宁夏，实现双向赋能、互利共赢。

## 结语

本次中澳商务对接交流会，实现了宁夏特色产业国际化突破与澳洲本土资源本土化赋能的深度融合。以政企联动为依托，以产业互补为核心，以数字出海为抓手，成功打通中澳经贸合作壁垒。

未来，双方将持续深耕务实合作、搭建长效交流平台，助力更多宁夏优质企业扬帆出海，让塞上特色产业立足澳洲、走向世界，持续释放中澳经贸合作新活力！

Recently, the China–Australia Business Matching Meeting, co-hosted by the **Oceania Business Council** and the **Ningxia Council for the Promotion of International Trade**, concluded successfully at Hexa Space in Melbourne. The event brought together Australian government guests, leading local enterprises and a delegation representing Ningxia's signature industries. Centred on industry dialogue, brand showcases and the signing of a strategic agreement, it established an international platform for Ningxia–Australia economic and trade connectivity, resource matching and long-term cooperation, building momentum for practical cooperation between enterprises of the two regions and for their industries to go global.

Moving beyond formalities, the event focused on **precise matching, practical win-win outcomes and long-term empowerment**. Through several core segments, including speeches by government and business leaders, industry sharing, company presentations, the signing of a strategic cooperation agreement and open networking, it efficiently opened channels for Ningxia's signature industries to go global, connected them precisely with Australian market, technology and channel resources, and promoted complementary strengths and two-way development between the two regions.

## Keynote Speeches Set the Tone and Chart a New Direction for China–Australia Cooperation

The speeches by the two principal guests set a tone of practical, in-depth and long-term joint development for economic and trade cooperation between the two regions, and outlined a clear path for Ningxia–Australia industrial collaboration.

### Oceania Business Council: Building a Two-Way Economic and Trade Bridge between China and Australia

Mr Sunny Sun, Executive President of the Oceania Business Council, delivered the opening address. He noted that China and Australia are highly complementary in economic and trade terms: Australia has a mature international market and business system, while Ningxia is home to high-quality signature industries such as wine, specialty agricultural products and health foods, and is an important window for the opening-up of north-west China.

He stressed that this matching meeting is **a new starting point for regular, institutionalised cooperation** between the two regions. Going forward, the Council will continue to leverage its strengths as an overseas platform to help Ningxia enterprises cultivate the Australian market and build international brands in every respect, while also bringing high-quality Australian resources to Ningxia, creating an economic and trade cooperation ecosystem in which both sides reach out to each other.

### CCPIT Ningxia: Taking Signature Industries to the Australian and International Markets

Mr Liang Wanrong, President of the Ningxia Council for the Promotion of International Trade, gave a focused presentation of Ningxia's core signature industries, highlighting advantageous sectors such as **wine from the eastern foothills of the Helan Mountains, goji berries and other agricultural products, green food processing and the health industry**, and introduced the ecological advantages and quality competitiveness of Ningxia's industries.

President Liang said that Australia is a core priority market for Ningxia enterprises going global. The purpose of leading a delegation of top local enterprises to Australia was to proactively connect with Australia's high-end market, advanced technology and mature trade channels. He expressed the hope that the two regions would establish a regular communication mechanism and deepen practical cooperation in areas such as trade connectivity, joint brand building and market development, so that Ningxia's high-quality signature products can take root in Australia and reach the world.

## Two-Way China–Australia Dialogue Unlocks New Momentum for Industrial Cooperation

The event assembled more than ten leading Ningxia enterprises covering core sectors including wine, specialty agricultural and food products, supply-chain trade and corporate marketing. Well-known brands such as the Silver Heights, Legacy Peak and Woerfeng wineries, together with Qilixiang, Houshengji and Baishi Hengxing, showcased Ningxia's signature products and industrial strength. Their high quality won broad recognition from Australian government and business guests, fully demonstrating the international competitiveness and development potential of Ningxia's signature industries.

Representatives of Australian local enterprises responded actively and engaged in precise matching, forming an efficient pattern of two-way interaction.

Mr William Yang, CEO of Hexa Group, shared his core views drawing on his experience of local operations in Australia, proposing that the keys to cross-border cooperation are **local adaptation, on-the-ground operations and long-term development**. As the venue partner for the event, Hexa Group has deep roots in the Melbourne market and is familiar with local business rules and consumer needs. Going forward, it will continue to draw on its venue, business-district and operational resources to provide one-stop support for China–Australia business matching and negotiations, project landing and brand promotion, helping Ningxia brands adapt quickly to the Australian market and overcome the practical challenges of establishing themselves overseas.

Zhao Songliang, Secretary-General of the Business Services Chapter of the Oceania Business Council, focused on cross-border business services, compliant operations and resource integration, analysing the opportunities and pain points of Ningxia–Australia cooperation and offering Ningxia enterprises professional, actionable guidance for going global, thereby opening a channel for business-services collaboration between the two regions.

Wang Lingmei, head of Baidefu Winery, drew on the development experience of the Australian wine industry to share proven models of international winery operations, brand internationalisation and regional collaboration, providing valuable reference for Ningxia's wine industry as it benchmarks against international standards, optimises its product portfolio and develops overseas markets.

## Digital E-commerce Empowerment Opens New Channels for Enterprises Going Global

To help Ningxia's traditional industries go global through digital channels, the event invited experienced Australian practitioners in digital technology and cross-border e-commerce to share hands-on experience, addressing the narrow channels, low efficiency and matching difficulties of traditional foreign trade and empowering the international transformation of enterprises.

Flex Huo, Director of Digitec IT Solutions, focused on digital cross-border operations and online promotion of overseas brands, sharing go-global solutions suited to small and medium-sized enterprises and proposing the use of digital technology to build an online cross-border sales matrix that helps Ningxia products reach Australian end consumers precisely.

Alex Xiao, founder of Fureeze, drew on Australian e-commerce rules and consumer characteristics to explain in detail the practical paths for cross-border e-commerce traffic operations and channel deployment. He said that Ningxia's high-quality agricultural, food and wine products closely match Australia's high-end consumer demand, and that mature e-commerce models can quickly open up incremental markets.

In addition, Peter Woodward, founder of BERRINGA, and Pan Xuefei, Managing Director of Aochun Simu Group, respectively shared the market positioning and cooperation models of Australia's health and premium food industries, broadening the boundaries of cooperation for multi-industry integrated development between Ningxia and Australia.

## Strategic Agreement Signed, Opening a New Chapter of Long-Term Win-Win Cooperation

At the event, the Oceania Business Council and the Ningxia Council for the Promotion of International Trade formally signed a **strategic cooperation memorandum of understanding (MOU)**, marking the formal establishment of a long-term and stable strategic partnership between the two regions.

The signing is an important milestone in regular, in-depth cooperation between the two regions. Going forward, the two parties will focus on core areas including industry matching, brand internationalisation, technology exchange, trade connectivity and talent exchange, continue to build a professional business matching platform, promote the large-scale internationalisation of Ningxia's signature industries and help high-quality Australian resources land in Ningxia, achieving two-way empowerment and mutual benefit.

## Conclusion

This China–Australia Business Matching Meeting achieved a deep integration of the international breakthrough of Ningxia's signature industries with the local empowerment of Australian resources. Relying on government–business collaboration, centred on industrial complementarity and driven by digital internationalisation, it successfully broke down barriers to China–Australia economic and trade cooperation.

Going forward, the two parties will continue to deepen practical cooperation and build a long-term exchange platform, helping more high-quality Ningxia enterprises set sail overseas so that Ningxia's signature industries can establish themselves in Australia and reach the world, continuously unleashing new vitality in China–Australia economic and trade cooperation.
